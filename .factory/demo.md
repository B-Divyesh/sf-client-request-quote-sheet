# Demo sandbox

Open `https://client-request-quote-sheet.sociobot.in/?demo=1` or choose **Try it with sample data** on the first screen.

The sample is a filled request sheet for Northline Studio. It includes a priced brand review, a priced landing page, and custom support priced on request. A visitor can edit the sheet, make a share link, prepare a client request, and export the packet.

Demo sheet, draft, packet, and license records use local-storage keys beginning with `demo:`. The demo never reads or writes the real `request-sheet:*` or `sb_license:*` records. **Reset demo** cancels pending demo saves, removes the demo keys, and reloads the original sample. **Start for real** removes the demo keys before opening the real workspace.
